## Process
First I chose an area of focus to build my research plan: a Customer Analysis Segmentation. Next, I created a decomposition that outlined what metrics or parameters I wanted to investigate and what to include in the dashboard. Last, I created the dashoard, and showcased the findings with conclusions and recommendations.

## Data
* ‘Zomato data.zip’: zip files of data related to the business
  * ‘food.csv’: food orders in the restaurants
  * ‘menu.csv’: food and beverage items served in the restaurants
  * ‘orders.csv’: order dates and sales in the restaurants
  * ‘restaurant.csv’: restaurants and locations
  * ‘users.csv’: user demographic information

## Assumptions
  * The ‘orders.csv’ sheet accurately reflects all order dates and sales for the relevant timeframe.
  * The ‘users.csv’ sheet accurately reflects all user demographic information.
  * Missing values or inconsistencies in the data are minimal and can be ignored.
  * The provided data format (columns, data types) is correct and consistent.


## Findings
* Total revenue from all customers over the 4 year period is $987 million dollars.
* Customers ranged from 18-33 years old with the top 5 age groups averaging the highest purchase behavior: 21-22, 27, 31 and 33.
* The highest spending income bracket are customers with a monthly income below $10k.
* Single customers spend more than married customers, but the highest spending demographic is ‘prefer not to say’.
* On average, women spend more than men.


## Recommendations
* **Total revenue from all customers over the 4 year period is $987 million dollars:**  To commemorate the revenue and celebrate their customers, Zomato may consider a promotion that gives back to the community.
* **Customers ranged from 18-33 years old with the top 5 age groups averaging the highest purchase behavior: 21-22, 27, 31 and 33:** Zomato may consider opening more locations in areas where this demographic traffic is highest, including college campuses, stadiums, recreation areas and music venues.
* **The highest spending income bracket are customers with a monthly income below $10k:** Zomato may consider increasing marketing budgets to offer promotions and incentives to students and young adults.
* **Single customers spend more than married customers, but the highest spending demographic is ‘prefer not to say’:** Zomato may consider expanding marketing to make this demographic feel more seen and welcomed.
* **On average, women spend more than men:** Zomato may consider offering a ladies night or promotion to women on some occasions.
