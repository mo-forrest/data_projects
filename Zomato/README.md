## Customer Analysis Segmentation

During my final project in Tripleten’s Business Intelligence Analytics program, I collaborated with a team. For my contribution, I completed a Customer Analysis Segmentation on an international restaurant and food delivery company to learn more about customers registered for the service. The purpose of this project was to explore the data and be creative.

## Description
  * 8 excel spreadsheets
  * Includes raw data, summary, analysis, and recommendations.

## Process
First I chose an area of focus to build my research plan: a Customer Analysis Segmentation. Next, I created a decomposition that outlined what metrics or parameters I wanted to investigate and what to include in the dashboard. Last, I created the dashoard, and showcased the findings with conclusions and recommendations.

## Data
* ‘Zomato data.zip’: zip files of data related to the business
  * ‘food.csv’: food orders in the restaurants
  * ‘menu.csv’: food and beverage items served in the restaurants
  * ‘orders.csv’: order dates and sales in the restaurants
  * ‘restaurant.csv’: restaurants and locations
  * ‘users.csv’: user demographic information

## Assumptions
  * The ‘orders.csv’ sheet accurately reflects all order dates and sales for the relevant timeframe.
  * The ‘users.csv’ sheet accurately reflects all user demographic information.
  * Missing values or inconsistencies in the data are minimal and can be ignored.
  * The provided data format (columns, data types) is correct and consistent.
